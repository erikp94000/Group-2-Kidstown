The Sun and the Rain

Both of them started to follow their instructions, and suddenly the Sun's rays were passing through the raindrops. This created an enormous, beautiful "Rain-Sun" in the sky. It looked like a bow, so to this day we call it a Rainbow!

...and that is how the rainbow was created. Hope that you liked the story!
