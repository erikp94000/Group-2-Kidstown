About this project

KidsTown was originally built in 1998 by students at the University of Colorado at Denver in partnership with the Tattered Cover Book Store. It was one of the earliest children's literacy sites on the web.

This version was modernized in 2026 by Group 2 of CS 3980 at MSU Denver. We preserved the site's original character while updating the underlying technology to run on modern browsers with no server needed.
